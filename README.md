# Jewar Safeno Jara

**Senior Lecturer & Researcher**  
Department of General Forestry | Mizan-Tepi University, Ethiopia

## About Me

I am a Senior Lecturer and Researcher specializing in ethnobotany, ethnoveterinary medicine, and biodiversity conservation. My work focuses on documenting indigenous ecological knowledge and the traditional use of medicinal and wild edible plants in rural Ethiopian communities.

**Education:**
- MSc in Biodiversity Conservation and Management
- BSc in Forest Resources Management

**Research Interests:**
- 🌿 Ethnobotany & Ethnoveterinary Medicine
- 🌍 Biodiversity Conservation
- 📚 Wild Edible Plants (WEPs)
- 🏞️ Indigenous Ecological Knowledge
- 🌳 Forest Resources Management
